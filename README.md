
# Super Store Sales Analysis 📊

A comprehensive data analysis project using **SQL** for business intelligence and **Power BI** for interactive storytelling. This project explores retail trends, customer value, and category performance to drive data-informed decisions.

## 🛠️ Project Workflow

1. **Data Preprocessing (Power BI/Power Query):**
   - Fixed data types for dates and numerical values.
   - Handled missing values and standardized categorical naming.
   - Added custom calculated columns for better time-series analysis.

2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (SQL):**
   - Wrote complex queries to identify top-performing customers and regional sales trends.
   - Validated data integrity between the raw CSV and the final model.
   - Created aggregations to double-check KPI accuracy (Total Sales, Orders, etc.).

3. **Data Visualization (Power BI):**
   - **Overview:** High-level KPIs tracking $2.25M in Total Sales across 9,789 orders.
   - **Category Analysis:** Deep dive into product performance across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.
   - **Customer Insights:** Ranking top 10 most valuable customers and segment-wise sales distribution.

## 🚀 Key Insights from the Dashboard

* **Sales Momentum:** Monthly sales show a significant peak in November and December, suggesting strong holiday-season demand.
* **Product Leadership:** **Technology** is the leading category ($326K from Phones alone), while **Furniture** (Chairs and Tables) follows closely.
* **Customer Value:** **Sean Miller** is the top-tier customer with $25K in lifetime value, followed by Tamara Chand ($19K).
* **Market Segments:** The **Consumer** segment is the powerhouse of the business, accounting for over **50.9%** of total sales, followed by Corporate (30.3%).

## 📂 Repository Contents
* `SuperStore_Sales_SQL.sql`: Comprehensive SQL script with analytical queries.
* `Store_Sales_Project.pbix`: Interactive Power BI Dashboard (3 Pages).
* `Store_Sales_Raw_data.csv`: The processed dataset.
